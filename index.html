S-Gen Energy & Infra Pvt Ltd

Established in 2012 with our operational headquarters in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, S-Gen Energy and Infra Pvt Ltd is a leading manufacturer, trader and supplier of a wide range of high-quality electrical products – Control Panels, Electrical Panel Boards, Solar solutions and much more.

As a quality-conscious organisation, every product passes through multiple quality checks performed by our team of quality analysts using ultra-modern equipment and technologies. Our products are known in the market for smooth finish, reliability, corrosion resistance and premium quality – at budget-friendly rates.

We are also a trusted service provider for Electrical Contracting, Electrical Wiring and allied services, delivered by specialists using the latest procedures. Superior products and services have helped us build a strong clientele base across the nation.

  • Panels built to safety standards
  • In-house quality analysts
  • Turnkey electrical contracting
  • Competitive, transparent pricing
